# Demande d'autorisation — Dar Al-Ma'rifa (Tajweed Quran, riwayah Warsh)

**Contexte** : brouillon d'e-mail à envoyer via la page "Contact us" de
easyquran.com (Dar Al-Ma'rifa, éditeur du Tajweed Quran, riwayah Warsh an
Nafi). Objectif : obtenir une autorisation écrite avant toute intégration
dans Coran Karim — même logique que l'autorisation déjà obtenue de Quran
Foundation (cf. `PUBLICATION_PLAY.md` §4.1). Sans réponse positive, ce
contenu n'est pas utilisé.

À adapter avant envoi : l'adresse de contact expéditrice (proposée :
`CoranKarim.Ia@gmail.com`), et la langue si un contact arabe est préférable.

**Objet** : Permission request — Warsh an-Nafi Tajweed Quran content for a non-commercial recitation app (Coran Karim)

Dear Dar Al-Ma'rifa team,

My name is Allae, and I am the developer of **Coran Karim**, a free Android
application that helps users memorize and recite the Quran. The app listens
to the user's recitation through an on-device speech recognition model and
checks it word by word against the Quranic text — no audio or personal data
ever leaves the device, and there are no ads or in-app purchases.

I came across your website (easyquran.com) and the Tajweed Quran in the
Warsh an-Nafi narration, and I would like to explore adding Warsh support to
the app, which today only covers the Hafs narration.

I am writing to ask for your permission to use content from your Warsh
Mushaf (specifically the Quranic text, and if possible the tajweed
color-coding) within Coran Karim, and to ask a few questions so we can do
this correctly. To be clear, the Quranic text itself would never be
altered in any way — it would be reproduced exactly as published, letter
for letter, with no changes of any kind. Our only question is about the
terms under which we may display it in the app and, where relevant,
extract it into a digital format the app can render:

1. Would Dar Al-Ma'rifa be willing to authorize this use, and under what
   conditions (attribution requirements, restrictions on redistribution,
   etc.)?
2. Is a formal license agreement required, or would written permission by
   email be sufficient, similar to arrangements we have with other Quranic
   content providers?
3. Is offline, on-device storage of the text within the app acceptable, or
   are there restrictions we should be aware of?
4. Is there someone specific on your team we should be in contact with for
   this kind of request?

We take content licensing seriously — every third-party source used in
Coran Karim (Quranic text, recitation audio, the speech recognition model)
has documented permission or an open license, and we would only proceed
with Warsh content after receiving your explicit authorization.

Thank you very much for your time and for the work Dar Al-Ma'rifa has done
over the years to make the Tajweed Quran accessible to millions of readers.
I look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,
Allae
Coran Karim
CoranKarim.Ia@gmail.com
